FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why wouldn't I just hire a full-time senior engineer or designer?

A senior designer or full-stack engineer in India costs upwards of ₹18L-₹30L/year plus benefits, equity, and recruitment fees. With our productized model, you pay a flat predictable subscription, get instant senior output, and can pause anytime during slow months.

How fast will my requests be delivered?

On average, most design screens, landing page sections, and Next.js components are completed within 48 hours or less. More complex architectural tasks (full database migrations, multi-step checkout flows) are broken into transparent milestones.

How does the pause feature work?

Billing cycles run for 31 days. If you only need 14 days of work and pause your subscription, you retain a 17-day credit that you can unpause whenever you need additional engineering sprints in the future.

What stack do you use for development?

We specialize in Next.js 15, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Aceternity UI, Supabase, Postgres, and Razorpay payment integrations.
